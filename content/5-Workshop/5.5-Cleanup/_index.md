#### Resource Cleanup

Congratulations! You have completed this lab.

To avoid unnecessary charges, open **AWS CloudFormation**, select the stack you created, and click **Delete**.

CloudFormation will automatically delete the resources managed by the stack.

#### Cleanup Steps
